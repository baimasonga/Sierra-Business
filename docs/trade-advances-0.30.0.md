# Deposits and advances - 0.30.0

Finance > Deposits & advances records customer deposits received and supplier advances paid, before an invoice exists. Select a registered customer/supplier, cash or bank ledger, positive NLe amount and payment reference. The payment uses today's open-period date. Read-only accounting staff can review balances; accounting read/write permissions are checked on each posting.

Customer deposits debit cash/bank and credit customer credits (2400). Supplier advances debit supplier refunds/credits (1300) and credit cash/bank. They do not create sales revenue or stock movements. The protected credit balances use the existing allocation/refund mechanisms: partial use, same-party invoices, outstanding-bill and available-credit limits, idempotent retries and immutable correction history. Reverse allocations/refunds before reversing their source. Cash movements join an open till through the existing journal capture. Source document, journal, credit and operation are committed atomically and queued together when sync capture is enabled; no new database schema or wire entity is introduced.

The dedicated register shows original and remaining balances. Customer/supplier credit registers also include these balances, and party statements show them as unapplied credit. Printed payment output identifies the deposit/advance and payment reference. The existing navigation confirmation protects unfinished forms. To apply a customer deposit, post a credit invoice for the same registered customer; normal credit-limit and hold checks still apply.

Scope: cash/bank payments in NLe using today's date. Physical cheque deposits and foreign-currency supplier-bill allocation use their existing workflows; this feature does not add cheque-advance handling or foreign-currency advances. Automatic recovery of this form is not added.
